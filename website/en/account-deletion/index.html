Delete your Koupa account

Available to customers and barbers

Last updated: July 29, 2026

You can delete your account and associated data in the app. If you cannot access the app, you can submit a request to Koupa by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Profile.

    Sign in to Koupa and go to your Profile page.

  2. Choose Delete Account.

    Select Delete Account and read the warning explaining the effect on upcoming appointments.

  3. Confirm deletion.

    After confirmation, the app submits the deletion request and signs you out.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in to the app, send a message from the email address registered to your account to:

saadisamir20200@gmail.com

Email subject: Koupa account deletion request

Include the registered phone number when your account uses one. We may request reasonable information to verify ownership and protect your data before processing the request. We will contact you if verification or more information is required.

What deletion does

  • Cancels upcoming Booked appointments and notifies affected users.
  • Deletes authentication credentials, device push tokens, onboarding drafts, legal-acceptance records, and personal notifications.
  • Deletes a barber's schedules and exceptions and deactivates services for new bookings.
  • Removes an owned shop and remaining shop memberships where applicable.
  • Unpublishes the public profile and replaces identifying fields such as name, phone, and location with deleted or anonymized values.

Data that may remain anonymized

Limited historical appointment, rating, and operational-integrity records may remain after direct identifiers have been removed. This keeps past records internally consistent. The account can no longer be used to sign in, and the profile is no longer published for booking.

Before deleting

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed in the app. If you only want to stop device notifications, disable Koupa's notification permission in Android settings instead of deleting the account.
