How it works

From search to the chair in four steps

A direct experience on your phone: no phone calls, no waiting on the line.

  1. 9:00

    Search

    Search by name or area, compare prices and ratings, or see the barbers closest to you.

  2. 10:00

    Choose

    Pick the service and the day, then a time from the available slots.

  3. 10:30

    Confirm

    Review the service, time and price, then confirm. You pay at the shop.

  4. 11:00

    Arrive

    When you arrive, scan the barber's code and your attendance is recorded.

Your appointment is saved and organized

Your next appointment is on Home and in My appointments, and you get a reminder one day and one hour before it.

  • One clear upcoming appointment at a time

Check in with a QR code

When you arrive, scan the barber's code in the app and your appointment becomes Present, then Completed after the service.

Booked→Present→Completed

  • Check-in opens half an hour before the appointment
  • One code, shown at the barber

FAQ

Questions you may have

Do I need an account to browse barbers?

No. You can browse and see prices and available times without an account; you only need one to confirm a booking.

How do I pay?

You pay at the shop in Algerian dinars, and the price is shown before you book.

How many appointments can I book?

One upcoming appointment at a time, so your plan stays clear.

What devices does it run on?

Koupa is an Android app for now, launching in Algeria.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account inside the app. Details are on the Account deletion page.
