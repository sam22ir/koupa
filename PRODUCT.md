# Koupa

## Register
Product. Arabic-first Android booking app UI (Expo + React Native). Design serves the booking job: find a barber, book a slot, show up, get confirmed via QR.

## Users
- **Customers** in Algeria booking barber appointments from their phone, often on mobile data, in a hurry, in Arabic (RTL). They pay at the shop in DZD.
- **Barbers** running their day: today's appointments, working hours, services, QR check-in, one shop membership.
- Two permanent roles only. Launch market: Algeria. Arabic-first with English-ready localization.

## Product purpose
Replace phone-tag barber scheduling with a clear booking record: search by name/city, pick service + time, arrive, scan QR, appointment marked Present → Completed. One active upcoming appointment per customer across all barbers.

## Brand personality
- Quiet paper, teal moments. Calm, trustworthy, precise. A well-kept barbershop ledger, not a neon crypto app.
- Deep Teal (#1A7A78) owns moment surfaces (stage/hero) only. Warm Gold (#E1A553) is a thin accent, never large fills. Charcoal Blue text (#323E4B) on Soft White paper (#F3F5F7).
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Initials instead of profile photos. DZD pricing.
- 150–250 ms motion on operational screens; no page-load choreography where users work.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards, purple/blue neon gradients, glassmorphism filler.
- Decorative orbs, watermark logos parked left, gold dashes under watermarks.
- English-first layouts awkwardly mirrored to RTL.
- AI-slop welcome screens: centered hero + gradient text + three equal feature cards.

## Strategic principles
- One primary action per first view. Secondary actions recede.
- Cards float on paper only when content needs a surface.
- RTL is native, not mirrored-afterthought: row order, icon direction, numerals, alignment.
- Welcome screen must serve both personas (customer / barber) without splitting into two apps visually; persona clarity before sign-up friction.
- Trust cues are concrete: confirmed status, real times, DZD prices, QR confirmation. No invented stats ("99.99% uptime").
